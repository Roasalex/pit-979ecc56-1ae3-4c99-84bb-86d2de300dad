Make a vertical video, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, timed to the voiceover file I attached. Nobody appears on camera. The whole video is ONE animated flowchart (a system map) on a near-black background. A slow virtual camera moves across it while the voice explains it.
LOOK
- Background #090909, flat. No stock footage, no photos, no AI people, no emojis.
- Text: white, bold, clean sans-serif. Headlines in caps with wide letter spacing.
- One accent color: gold #CFA54E. Use it for the box being explained, the hand-drawn circle, arrows that light up, and the button at the end.
- Red #E5484D only for "breaks" stamps and the X. Green #3DBE7B only for BOOKED and JOB DONE.
- Boxes: dark charcoal #151515 rounded rectangles, thin #2A2A2A border, a small number in the corner, a bold white title, one tiny gray line under it.
- Arrows: thin gray lines with small arrowheads. An arrow turns gold when the voice moves along it.
THE MAP (always these 9 boxes, top to bottom in a gentle S shape so it fits a phone)
1 AD - your phone clip, city + price
2 FORM - short, on their phone
3 TEXT BACK - under 60 seconds
4 FOLLOW-UPS - day 1, 3, 7
5 BOOKED - on your calendar
6 REMINDER - before the job
7 JOB DONE - paid
8 REVIEW ASK - after the job
9 MONDAY SCORECARD - leads, booked jobs, money booked
Plus one curved green arrow from box 7 back up to box 1, labeled "paid jobs teach the ads".
MOTION
- A box appears the moment the voice names it, never before. The arrow into it draws first.
- Slow push-in on the box being explained. A hand-drawn gold ellipse draws around it and holds while the voice talks about it. Pull back to show the whole map at each section change.
- Something new happens on screen every 2 to 5 seconds.
- Headline: the key phrase of the moment sits in the top third in big white caps, 2 to 5 words, then fades when the next one comes.
- Stamps slam on at a slight angle where the shot list says so.
CAPTIONS
- Word by word, white, bottom center, 1 to 3 words at a time, synced to the voice. Never over a box title. Numbers as digits: "60 seconds", "day 1, 3 and 7", "$10,264".
END CARD (last 2.5 seconds)
- The whole map, small. A gold button that says: DM "LEADS". Under it in white: elevate-mk-tg.com. No logo animation.
NEVER
- No client or business names, no phone numbers, no screenshots of texts or ad accounts, no faces.
- No number that is not in my script. Where the shot list says EXAMPLE, show the word EXAMPLE in small gray text next to the numbers.
- Music: a low steady beat or none. Never louder than the voice.
Below is the shot list for this video. Follow it in order.

18. "we set the whole thing up for you. About a week." - Whole map, all stamps gone, every box lit. White stamp: WE SET IT UP. ABOUT A WEEK.
19. "We don't promise a number..." - Headline: NO PROMISED NUMBERS. EVERY NUMBER, EVERY MONDAY.
20. "That's the machine." - End card.
