1. If you own a local service business in Colorado — tint, flooring, cleaning, a trade — and you're paying for ads
   but your calendar still has holes, give me two minutes. I'm going to show you exactly how this works and who it's
   for. So if we get on a call, you already know.
2. First, who this is for. You're already doing jobs. People leave you good reviews. You could take five, ten more a
   week and you'd be fine. And either you answer your phone fast, or you want a system that does it for you. Who it's
   not for: a brand-new business with no reviews yet, or anyone hoping five hundred bucks a month fixes everything.
   If that's you — no hard feelings. Don't book the call yet.
3. Here's what I see over and over. Most owners think they have an ad problem. They don't. They have a follow-up
   problem. Someone fills out your form at eleven. You're on a job. You call back at six — and they already booked
   whoever texted back first. The lead wasn't bad. It was late.
4. So here's what we actually do, step by step. Week one, we build your system. Every lead lands in one place. The
   second someone fills out the form, they get a text — under sixty seconds — with their name and the exact thing they
   asked about. If they don't answer, follow-ups go out on day one, three and seven. A reminder before the job. A
   review ask after. You don't touch any of it. You become the shop that texts back first. That's the whole game.
5. Then the ads. You don't need a film crew. You film two minutes on the job with your phone and send it to me. I
   turn it into the ad.
6. Hook in the first second. Your city and your price on screen. Captions, because most people watch with the sound
   off. One clear thing to do. Ads are live in about a week, only in your zip codes. Your ad spend goes straight to
   Meta — we never mark it up.
7. Every Monday you get a scorecard: leads, booked jobs, money booked. I judge the ads on booked jobs, not clicks.
8. Your part is two things. Answer the jobs that book, and tell me what each one paid. That's it.
9. For a tint shop in Aurora we work with, that's six hundred seventy-nine leads from about sixty-four hundred dollars
   in ads — around nine-fifty a lead.
10. Three ways to work with us. Leads — nine hundred a month plus a six-hundred setup, month to month, you answer your
    own leads. Growth Partner — six hundred a month plus a piece of the jobs we bring you, so you pay on results. And
    Full Service, from three thousand — ads, a video shoot every month, your website, Google, every review answered.
11. On the fifteen-minute call I'll ask you three things: what a job is worth to you, how many more you can take, and
    who answers the phone. Then I'll tell you straight which plan fits — even if the answer is none of them. Book it below.
