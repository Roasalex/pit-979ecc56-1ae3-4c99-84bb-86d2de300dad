# Style reference: "notecards-contractor"

**Not a brand.** A reel the creator likes (Bull & Built, "Contractors, not on Google Maps?" and "If you're a
contractor"), measured Oct 2026, kept as an editing style to copy. Borrow the pacing, caption treatment, layouts
and motifs; never the Bull & Built name, logo, tagline, URL or CTA. Colours below are theirs: swap in the
creator's brand colours from `brand/<brand>/BRAND.md`. Theme code: `theme.tsx`.

Who: helps contractors (concrete, striping, sealcoating, landscaping, welding) show up on Google and get
leads. Voice: plain-spoken, from the job site, "real tips you can use today". Tagline: *Be the Bull. Lead the Market.*

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| red | `#E1362E` | tags behind serif lines, URL tag, pins, corner brackets |
| yellow | `#F5D355` | sticky notes, caption emphasis word |
| ink | `#0C090F` | dark grounds (statement panel, map) |
| cream | `#EAE4DA` | light grounds, end card |
| paper | `#F7F4EE` | index cards (red top rule, blue ruled lines) |
| pen | `#253D97` | handwriting on cards |

Never over video: large areas of ink or cream; those are full-screen grounds only.

## Type (best matches from low-res exports; replace with the real files if they exist)

- Captions and statements: **Inter** 700/800, light lines in 400
- Accent lines, tags, taglines: **Instrument Serif Italic**
- Labels and URL: **JetBrains Mono** 500/700, wide letter-spacing, uppercase
- Handwriting on cards and the CTA sticky: **Kalam** 700
- Wordmark: **Archivo Black** (the real logo lockup should replace it)

## Captions

White Inter Bold ~64px, sentence case, **no outline**, soft shadow. One emphasis word per line in yellow
(the noun that matters: "main", "photos", "active", "contractors"). 1 to 6 words, up to two lines, centred on the
chest (~y1215 on the office framing). This deliberately differs from the skill's outlined TikTok default.

## Hooks and titles

- Hook: bold black Inter line + Instrument Serif italic line under it, top third, slightly rotated
  ("Contractors, / not on Google Maps?"), sometimes with a big red word ("INVISIBLE") breaking in.
- Red tags: stacked serif-italic lines on red blocks, last line bigger ("one of these / 3 things").
- Cold open on cream: small spaced mono line + big red condensed word ("IF YOU'RE A / CONTRACTOR").

## Motifs

- Pinned index cards and yellow stickies for numbered points; ✗ wrong / ✓ right in handwriting; earlier cards
  shrink to the edges and get tied together with **red string** as the list builds.
- Corkboard recap of all cards before the CTA.
- Dark statement panel: dotted grid, red corner brackets, "I know." lead-in, bold/light word mix,
  footer `BULL & BUILT · FROM THE JOB SITE`.
- Dark map ground with search bar typing "contractors near me" and a #1 pin with the bull logo.
- Floating 3D phone/site mockups of client sites ("look like the TOP CREW online").
- Pacing: a cut, card or graphic change every ~1.1 to 1.7s.

## CTA and end card (their pattern; use the creator's own CTA and logo)

`Comment "SITE"` (yellow sticky, Kalam) / "Free Google profile check". End card on cream: logo, wordmark,
tagline, red `BULLANDBUILT.COM` tag, sticky CTA.
