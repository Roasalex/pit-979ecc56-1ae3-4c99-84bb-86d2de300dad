# Elevate: brand sheet

**Status: draft, waiting on approval.** Colours and type read from elevate-mk-tg.com's own stylesheet (Nov 2026);
character art from the creator's cartoon avatar scenes. Theme code: `theme.tsx`.

Who: Elevate, Denver. Ads, websites and automated follow-up for auto shops and local service businesses (tint, PPF &
ceramic, detailing, flooring, house cleaning, barbershops, movers, contractors). Site headline: "More booked jobs.
Every month. Without chasing leads." Voice: straight, no hype, "proof, not promises".

## Colour (from the site's CSS variables)

| Token | Hex | Site variable |
|---|---|---|
| ink | `#030303` | `--background` |
| card | `#080808` / panel `#0F0F0F` | `--card` / `--secondary` |
| paper | `#F5F3F0` | `--foreground` |
| red | `#F52F39` | `--primary` (one accent, used sparingly) |
| grey | `#989898` | `--muted-foreground` |

The cartoon scenes bring their own night palette (navy sky, teal city lights, amber rim light). Graphics stay in the
site's black / off-white / red so the brand reads the same on the site and in the ads; red is the one emphasis colour.

## Type

Site: `"Helvetica Neue", "Neue Haas Grotesk", Arial, sans-serif` (system fonts, no web font). In video we use
**Inter** (bundled, closest free match) unless the creator has Neue Haas Grotesk licence files to drop in.

## Captions

White Inter 800 with a black outline (the skill's default caption), the key word in red. 1-3 words per line.
Placed on the lower third of the cartoon scenes, clear of the character's face.

## Look and motifs

- The creator's cartoon avatar (curly hair, black sunglasses, black suit, silver watch) in night scenes with the
  Denver skyline and a black Corvette. One scene per beat; slow push-ins (Ken Burns) so stills feel alive.
- Black cards with a thin red rule for numbers and steps; proof shown as the site does: number big, label small.
- Reusable graphics from `src/shared/` restyled black/red: DataFlow ("YOUR CRM"), StatCount, SegmentMeter, QuoteFrame.

## Proof (only these, as published on the site)

- Precision Tintz (Aurora, CO): 75 booked jobs in 60 days, $7 per booked appointment; Jared: "We used to have only
  3-4 ceramic coating jobs done in a month and now we do over 50 jobs a month."
- Detail Edge (DeWitt, MI): 30 inbound leads on a $200 spend; 60 ceramic packages sold at $599 in a month.
- Apex Motorworks: 80 booked jobs, $6 per booked appointment. OnSite Flooring: 20 high-ticket leads in 30 days.

## CTA

"Book a call" / "Book a Free Strategy Call" (site). In the ad: book the 15-minute call below.
